Hey Harshwardhan

Some days are meant to be light. Full of colour, movement, laughter. And nothing that needs to be rushed.

Will you be my date?

No pressure.
Just curiosity, warmth,
and letting the day feel easy.

🦒 Zoo walks

📸 Laughing at bad photos

I really liked our first date.
I want to know you better —
slowly, honestly.
